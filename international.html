COMMUNITY OUTREACH

Eye Go International

A community-focused vision outreach initiative dedicated to bringing the gift of clearer vision to communities in need around the world.

OUR MISSION

Clearer vision, shared widely.

Jamaica · Colombia

Through international outreach efforts, we have traveled to Jamaica and Colombia, providing vision screenings and distributing eyeglasses to children and adults who may not otherwise have access to quality vision care.

Our mission is simple: to help people see better, live better, and experience the world more clearly.

Eye Go International provides glasses through a combination of gently used donated eyewear and new glasses, helping us extend the life of quality eyewear while making a meaningful difference in someone’s everyday life.
